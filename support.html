How the game works

When do the questions drop?
Every day at midnight Eastern, five yes-or-no questions about the following day. Everyone gets the same five.
When does a question lock?
Each question locks on its own, at the time shown on its card: an all-day question at midnight, a game at kickoff, the market at the opening bell. You can lock in a few calls now and the rest later.
How does a question settle?
From the public source named on the card, automatically, at the time shown. If the source gives no answer, the question is voided and counts for nobody.
What is the star?
One call per edition can be doubled. It is worth two points if right, nothing extra if wrong.
How are the crowd and the AI scored?
On the same questions you called. The crowd's call is the majority; the AI makes its own call on every question before anyone plays.
What breaks a streak?
A day with no calls locked in. Your best streak is kept.
How do leagues work?
Start one and share the six-character code, or join with a friend's. Points are counted Monday to Sunday, New York time, and the standings reset every Monday.
Is there money involved?
No. Points only. Nothing to buy, nothing to wager, nothing paid out.

Your data

There is no account. The app makes up a random id on first run and keeps your calls, scores and streak against it. Deleting the app's data, or deleting the app, ends it. The details are in the privacy policy.
