Tomorrow

Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version

Tomorrow has no accounts. The app makes up a random id when you first open it, and your calls, scores, streak and league membership are kept against that id. You can add a display name for leagues; nothing else about you is collected. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared with advertisers, and there is no tracking.

What is stored

  • An anonymous id. A random identifier the app generates on first run and keeps on your phone. It is not your device's identifier, not your Apple ID, and not tied to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your play. The yes-or-no calls you lock in, which one you doubled, your scores, your streak and your results for the last 60 days.
  • A display name, if you add one. Up to 24 characters, typed on the Leagues screen. It is shown to the other members of the leagues you are in, and nowhere else.
  • League membership. Which leagues your id belongs to.

This is stored on a hosted database (Convex) that the developer runs, in the United States. The crowd split shown in the app is a count of yes and no calls; it is never shown per person.

What is not collected

No email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier, or analytics. The app has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no crash reporter. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Third parties

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts when it starts. Like any web request, that sends your IP address to Google; Google's handling of it is covered by Google's privacy policy. Questions are settled from public sources (weather records, scores, market closes, headlines); those sources receive requests from the developer's server, never from your phone.

Sharing

If you share a scorecard or a league invite, the app hands the text or image to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, or clear its data, and the id on your phone is gone; nothing can connect the remaining server records to you. To have those records removed as well, email trevsky1@gmail.com with your league code or display name, and they will be deleted.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and asks for no personal information. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect anything from them beyond the anonymous play described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
